# Returns and Refund Policy

## Returns
You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.

## How to get a refund
To request a refund, email support@harbourhill.ie with your order number. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the item.

## Faulty items
If an item is faulty, we will repair, replace or refund it free of charge in line with Irish consumer law.
