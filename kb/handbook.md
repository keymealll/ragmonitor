# Harbour & Hill Outdoor — Customer Handbook

## About us
Harbour & Hill Outdoor is a family-run outdoor gear shop in Cork, Ireland. We sell hiking boots, rain jackets, tents and cycling gear in store and online.

## Opening hours
Our Cork shop is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm, and Sunday 12pm to 5pm. Online orders can be placed at any time.

## Delivery
We deliver anywhere in Ireland within 2 to 4 working days. Delivery is free on orders over €60, otherwise it costs €5.95. We also ship to Northern Ireland and the UK for €12.

## Contact
For any question about an order, email support@harbourhill.ie or call 021 555 0142 during opening hours.

## Boot fitting
Book a free boot fitting in store. A staff member will measure your feet and help you choose hiking boots that suit your walking.
