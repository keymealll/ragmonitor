# Changes, cancellations and refunds

## Changing your dates
You can move a trip to new dates in the app up to 7 days before departure. Any difference in price is charged or paid back to your card.

## How to get a refund
Cancel in the app at least 14 days before departure for a full refund, paid back to your original payment method within 5 working days. Inside 14 days the hotel is refunded and the flights are not.

## When an airline cancels
If an airline cancels your flight, Skerry rebooks you on the next flight or refunds the whole trip, in line with EU passenger rights.
