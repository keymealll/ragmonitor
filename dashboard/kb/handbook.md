# Skerry help centre

## About Skerry
Skerry is a travel app based in Cork that plans and books short trips from Irish airports. One booking covers your flights, a hotel and a plan for each day.

## Booking a trip
Pick a destination, your dates and how many people are travelling. Skerry holds the price for 20 minutes while you pay, and your booking reference arrives by email straight away.

## Baggage
Every Skerry flight includes one cabin bag up to 10 kg. A checked bag of 20 kg costs €35 each way and can be added in the app up to 24 hours before departure.

## Destinations
This season Skerry flies from Cork and Dublin to Lisbon, Porto, Barcelona, Paris, Amsterdam and Kraków. Paris trips leave Dublin every day and Cork on Fridays.

## Contact
For any question about a booking, email support@skerry.ie or use the chat on the website. The support team answers between 8am and 8pm every day.
